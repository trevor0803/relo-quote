02 / ARTIST
The artist

Old-school training. No interest in doing the obvious thing.

Jon started in New Jersey with the kind of apprenticeship where you learned the shop before you earned the machine. He apprenticed again after moving to Florida, rebuilt his approach, and kept going.

Fifteen-plus years later, the work is still drawn for one person at a time. Black and grey is the language. Dark fantasy, mythology, portraiture and architectural structure are the vocabulary.

The internet got bigger. The sessions did not get generic.

“The same guy you see on the internet.”

Dry humor. Straightforward takes. Serious craft. That part of the brand is not a campaign.
